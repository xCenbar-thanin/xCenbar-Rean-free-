២. កម្រិត B2 — Upper-Intermediate (មធ្យមកម្រិតខ្ពស់)

B2 = អ្នកអាច យល់គំនិតសំខាន់នៃអត្ថបទស្មុគស្មាញ (រួមទាំងការពិភាក្សាបច្ចេកទេសក្នុងជំនាញខ្លួន) ប្រាស្រ័យទាក់ទង ដោយស្ទាត់ និងឯកឯង ជាមួយអ្នកនិយាយដើមដោយគ្មានការលំបាកច្រើនសងខាង សរសេរអត្ថបទច្បាស់លាស់ និងលម្អិត ហើយពន្យល់ទស្សនៈ ព្រមទាំងគុណសម្បត្តិ/គុណវិបត្តិ។

Present deduction

must = ប្រាកដ ~95% (វិជ្ជមាន) · might / may / could = ប្រហែល ~50% · can't = ប្រាកដថាមិនពិត ~95%។ ❌ mustn't មិនប្រើសម្រាប់ការស្មាន។

He's been working all day. He must be tired.
គាត់ធ្វើការពេញមួយថ្ងៃ។ គាត់ច្បាស់ជាហត់។
She might be at the library.
គាត់ប្រហែលនៅបណ្ណាល័យ។
That can't be Sokha — he's in Japan.
នោះមិនអាចជាសុខាទេ — គាត់នៅជប៉ុន។

Past deduction — modal + have + V3

must have V3 · might/may/could have V3 · can't/couldn't have V3។

She's not here. She must have missed the bus.
គាត់មិននៅទីនេះ។ គាត់ច្បាស់ជាខកឡានក្រុង។
He can't have stolen it — he was with me all evening.
គាត់មិនអាចលួចវាទេ — គាត់នៅជាមួយខ្ញុំពេញមួយល្ងាច។

should have / could have — ការរិះគន់ & ឱកាសខកខាន

should have V3 = គួរតែបានធ្វើ (តែមិនបានធ្វើ) · could have V3 = អាចធ្វើបាន (តែមិនបានធ្វើ)។

You should have told me earlier!
អ្នកគួរតែប្រាប់ខ្ញុំមុននេះ!
We could have won if we had played better.

📖 Angkor: A Living Heritage

Angkor, which was the capital of the Khmer Empire from the 9th to the 15th centuries, is one of the most important archaeological sites in Southeast Asia.

Angkor Wat, built in the early 12th century by King Suryavarman II, was originally dedicated to the Hindu god Vishnu. Its walls, covered with detailed carvings, tell stories from ancient epics.

The site, listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1992, attracts visitors from all over the world. Teams working with the APSARA National Authority are restoring temples damaged by time and nature.

Local families living inside the park still farm the land, which makes Angkor a 'living' heritage site.

📖 Model Essay: Should students wear school uniforms?

In many countries, including Cambodia, students are required to wear uniforms. While some people believe uniforms limit self-expression, I would argue that their benefits clearly outweigh the drawbacks.

Firstly, uniforms reduce social pressure. Students from poorer families do not have to compete with classmates in expensive clothes; therefore, everyone feels more equal.

Secondly, uniforms save time and money. Parents do not need to buy many outfits, and children can get ready quickly in the morning.

Admittedly, uniforms can prevent young people from expressing their personality. However, schools can allow creativity in other ways, such as clubs, art and music.

In conclusion, despite some limitations, school uniforms promote equality and practicality, so I believe they should remain compulsory.
